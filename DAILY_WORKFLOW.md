# Daily Workflow

Plan for 60–120 minutes.

1. **Preview (10 min):** read the day's `README.md`; write down what you expect to learn.
2. **Learn (20–30 min):** study `notes.md` and the linked lesson/video material you choose.
3. **Implement (20–30 min):** write the core structure or algorithm in `examples/`.
4. **Practice (20–40 min):** complete at least two prompts from `problems/` without looking at a solution first.
5. **Reflect (5–10 min):** capture complexity, edge cases, and one mistake in `notes.md`.
6. **Track:** update the corresponding checkbox in `PROGRESS_TRACKER.md`.

## Definition of Done

A day is complete when you can explain the technique, implement its basic form, state complexity, solve at least one problem unaided, and record a short review note.

## Weekly Review

At the end of each six-day module, redo one previously solved problem from memory, review your error log, and complete the module's review challenge.
